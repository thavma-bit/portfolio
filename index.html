About

Faith.
Design.
Purpose.

I'm Mirakle Gaase — a Philosophy graduate turned visual storyteller, based in Makurdi, Benue State, Nigeria. I design at the intersection of faith, culture, and technology — with a creative voice shaped by Tiv heritage and the gospel.

My work spans brand identities, church media, book covers, and motion content. I'm also building tools that bring AI into ministry — from Bible projection systems to podcast infrastructure for gospel communities.

Legally Miracle, creatively Mirakle — the 'k' is intentional.
